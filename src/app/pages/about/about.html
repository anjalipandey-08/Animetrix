URBAN ANIMAL LIFE
OUR PURPOSE

Connecting people, observations and urban spaces.

Animals are an important part of urban environments. However, their needs can often be overlooked during everyday urban development.

Animetrix provides a digital platform where observations can be documented, community experiences can be collected and animal-friendly infrastructure can be explored.
